## Post

I spent the weekend building IrishNightlife, a way to answer "where are we going tonight?" for Cork.

You pick who you are with, the vibe, the budget, and how early tomorrow starts. It comes back with two or three real venues that are actually on tonight, not a generic list of pubs.

The first version is Cork only: venues, what's on, a map, and the planner. Venue owners can sign up and see their own listings. Dublin and payments come after this is in people's hands.
